* Using standard vacuums: Regular vacuums lack HEPA filtration. They simply blow fine dust back into the air. This makes the problem worse. * Dry dusting: Wiping surfaces dry only moves dust around. It doesn't remove it. It just makes it airborne again. * Neglecting vertical surfaces: Walls, ceilings, and high shelves collect substantial dust. People often forget these areas. * Insufficient air filtration: Relying on open windows isn't enough. You need dedicated air scrubbers.
