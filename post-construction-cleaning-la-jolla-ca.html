Navigating Common Post-Construction Cleaning Mistakes

Many assume post-construction cleaning is similar to a regular deep clean. This is a critical misconception. Failing to recognize the unique challenges leads to common errors.

A significant mistake is underestimating dust penetration. Construction dust is insidious. It infiltrates HVAC systems, settles inside cabinets, and coats every surface. Generic cleaning products are another pitfall. Using abrasive compounds or high-pH chemistry can etch natural stone. This strips sealants, common in high-end La Jolla homes. Neglecting ventilation systems allows dust to recirculate. This undermines all other cleaning efforts. Rushing the process also guarantees a subpar result. True post-construction cleaning demands patience and precision.
