Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do coastal weather conditions in San Diego affect my interior home maintenance needs?

San Diego's marine layer brings moisture and microscopic airborne sea salt into homes, especially in coastal neighborhoods like La Jolla, Coronado, Pacific Beach, and Ocean Beach. Salt air accelerates the oxidation of unlacquered metals and reacts with dust to form abrasive grime on surfaces. Regular, technical cleaning using HEPA filtration and pH-neutral damp wiping prevents salt grit from scratching delicate floor finishes or corroding polished fixtures.

Why is vinegar dangerous to use on natural stone countertops and floors?

Vinegar is acetic acid with a low pH of approximately 2.5. Natural stones like marble, limestone, travertine, and certain quartzites contain calcium carbonate. When vinegar touches these surfaces, the acid chemically dissolves the calcium on contact, causing instant surface etching, loss of shine, and rough micro-pitting that requires professional stone restoration to repair.

What is the difference between standard domestic house cleaning and technical high-end maid services?

Standard house cleaning focuses primarily on surface tidying, dusting, and general steam-clean using off-the-shelf consumer products. Technical high-end maid services utilize trained specialists, material science, specialized equipment (like commercial dry-vapor steam machines and HEPA backpack vacuums), and color-coded cross-contamination prevention systems. They customize chemical pH levels based on surface porosity to preserve luxury architectural materials long term.

How does high-heat dry-vapor steam protect natural tile and grout lines?

Commercial dry-vapor steam systems heat water to over 275°F, producing low-moisture thermal shock energy. This dry steam breaks the physical surface tension holding oil, grime, and mold inside porous grout lines without requiring harsh chemicals or excess water. Because it uses less than 5% moisture, dry steam cleans deeply without soaking subfloors or damaging adjacent delicate natural stone borders.
