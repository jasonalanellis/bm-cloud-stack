Common Mistakes in DIY Pet Stain Removal

Many homeowners attempt to clean pet accidents themselves. They often use incorrect products or techniques. This frequently worsens the problem.

Over-Wetting Carpets

Applying too much liquid pushes contaminants deeper. This can damage carpet backing or subflooring. It also promotes mold and mildew growth. Drying becomes a prolonged challenge.

Using Household Cleaners

Many common household cleaners contain harsh chemicals. These can set stains permanently. They might also damage carpet fibers or finishes. Bleach, for example, can irreversibly discolor fabrics. Ammonia-based cleaners attract pets to re-mark the same spot.

Skipping Odor Neutralization

Surface cleaning might remove the visible stain. It rarely eliminates the underlying odor source. Without proper odor neutralization, the smell will return. Pets also possess a much stronger sense of smell. What seems clean to you might still signal a "bathroom" to them.
